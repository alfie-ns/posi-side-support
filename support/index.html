Help with your private positive-moment notebook.

Opening-date check in version 1.0

We have identified a configuration issue that can prevent a sealed notebook opening after an iPhone restart or clock change. Version 1.0.1 is being prepared with a working date-confirmation service. It is not yet available on the App Store. Keep the app and its data installed; reinstalling will not fix this issue. Your stored opening date does not need to be reset.

Quick help

  • The keyboard is covering the app: tap outside the editor, swipe the keyboard down or switch tabs.
  • A saved moment is still visible: each capture has a 30-second Undo period before it seals.
  • Time verification is required: connect briefly, then tap Verify time and open. No journal text is sent by this check.
  • The app asks you to authenticate again: decrypted entries are cleared when Posi-Side leaves the foreground. Use Face ID or your passcode to return to an active Review Window.
  • An imported item is under Unknown Date: its text did not contain a supported date heading. The original text remains unchanged.
  • Action Button setup: add Posi-Side’s Log Moment control or shortcut in iOS Settings, then assign it to the Action Button.

Protect your Recovery Key

Never send your twelve Posi-Side recovery words to support. Posi-Side cannot retrieve them and support will never ask for them.

Contact support

Email support@anythingaloud.com for help or Android test access. Do not send passwords, journal entries or recovery words. For Android, include the Android version and Posi-Side version if installed. Android test privacy notice.

Open a private-data-free support request. Include the Posi-Side version, iOS version and a description of what happened. Do not include journal entries or recovery words.

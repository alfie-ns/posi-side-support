Posi-Side

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Information stored on your iPhone

Posi-Side works without an account. Your encrypted journal entries, selected journal dates, cycle timing and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Cryptographic keys and recovery material are stored locally in the iOS Keychain. Posi-Side does not operate an account database and the developer does not receive your journal text.

Device authentication is performed by iOS. Posi-Side does not receive or store your Face ID data or device passcode.

Network use

After an iPhone restart or a suspicious clock change, Posi-Side may make an HTTPS request to an Apple endpoint to obtain the current time before opening matured notes. That request contains no journal text, note dates, recovery material or Posi-Side account identifier. Apple receives ordinary network information such as the IP address and user agent under Apple’s privacy terms.

The forthcoming 1.0.1 update uses a dedicated time-confirmation service hosted by Cloudflare. When you request a check, it receives a random notebook identifier and a fresh random challenge, then returns a signed timestamp. It receives no journal text, selected journal dates, recovery words, advertising identifier or account details. The network provider processes ordinary connection information, including your IP address. The service uses short-lived, minute-specific hashed network counters to limit abuse; it does not write request or response bodies to application logs or a journal database.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Posi-Side schedules generic local reminders on your iPhone. Notification text does not include journal content.

Imports, exports and backups

Posi-Side reads a text import only after you select it. An exported .posiside file is encrypted and is written only to a location you choose. Exported files remain in Files, cloud storage, messages, email or another destination until you delete them there.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes its journal database and app container. iOS may retain Posi-Side cryptographic Keychain items after uninstall; they do not contain plaintext journal entries but can include recovery material. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them there.

No tracking or AI processing in this release

The first public release does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics, sale of personal information or third-party AI processing. If a future update enables AI Coach, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before release.

Children

Posi-Side is a general personal-reflection tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated service.

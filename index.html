The whole ritual

Write it. Leave it. See it differently.

Record what actually happened without deciding what it meant. A few words are enough. Over time, those easy-to-dismiss moments become a collection you can come back to.

Before you begin

A little more about the wait.

Can I keep writing while my notes are sealed?

Yes. Keep capturing moments while you wait. Each new note has its own 30-second undo period before it seals.

What happens when my notebook opens?

You get a seven-day Review Window to revisit the collection. Afterwards, the notes return to the protected archive. The app also has a once-in-three-years Review Day that opens sealed history for 24 hours. Enable reminders if you would like help remembering the opening date.

Does it need the internet?

You can capture moments offline. After an iPhone restart or a clock change, a brief internet connection may be needed to confirm the opening date. That check does not send your journal text. We are preparing a fix for this check in version 1.0; read the current release note.

Is this a subscription?

No. The current UK App Store price is £3.99, paid once. There is no subscription attached to that purchase. Prices vary by country; the App Store shows your local price before you buy.

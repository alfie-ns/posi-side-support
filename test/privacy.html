Your invitation request

If you submit the form, Anything Aloud Ltd receives the email address you use with Google Play and your agreement to be contacted about this test. We use these to review your request, arrange access and ask for feedback. There is no newsletter subscription. An invitation is not guaranteed.

The request is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database accessible to the project operator. It includes the request time, consent notice version and, only if you allow measurement, a campaign source label. Request records expire after 30 days and are removed by daily cleanup; requests also trigger cleanup. Hosting recovery backups may retain deleted records for the provider’s recovery period. If you join testing, Google processes your test access under its own terms. Any further contact is handled through the support mailbox.

To withdraw or request deletion, email support@anythingaloud.com from the address you used. Include only that you want your Posi-Side test request deleted. Do not send journal entries or recovery words. Support correspondence is handled separately from the form database and may be retained while needed to resolve your request.

Optional campaign counts

Counts are off unless you choose Allow counts. This records page views and button taps grouped by day and one of a small set of campaign labels. We do not put your email, full URL, search terms, referrer, IP address or a visitor identifier in the counter database. There are no cookies, device-storage identifiers, ad pixels or fingerprinting. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control keep optional counts off.

Your choice lives only in page memory. Choose No thanks to stop further optional events; counts already combined into totals cannot be separated. Successful unique request submissions are counted as an operational total even when optional measurement is off; their source is then unattributed. Aggregate counters are kept for 90 days. These counts are not unique visitors or app installs.

GitHub Pages serves the page and Cloudflare handles form and count requests. They necessarily process connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect these services under their own terms. Application request-body logging is disabled. This is not a promise of anonymity.

Your Android notebook

In the Android 1.0.0 test release, moment text is encrypted on your phone. There is no Posi-Side account, advertising, behavioural analytics or AI Coach in the app. The invitation form is separate from your notebook. Device authentication is handled by Android; we do not receive your screen-lock credentials or biometric data.

Capture and sealing work offline. After a restart or clock change, a user-requested time check can contact Google’s standard Android connectivity endpoint with a bodyless HTTPS request. It sends no journal text, note dates, recovery words, archive or app-generated identifier. Network providers receive ordinary connection metadata.

Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Keep your Recovery Key and an encrypted export if you need to restore on another phone. Anything Aloud cannot recover a lost key or an unexported notebook. The time gate supports the wait you chose; it does not guarantee protection against a modified app, operating system or compromised device.

Testing is for adults. Use sample text and report only how the app behaved, not what you wrote.
